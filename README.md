Entry for the make.fun bounty "One-Line Slogan for a Solana Wallet Agent": a single slogan of at most twenty words for a Solana wallet agent.

> Your Solana wallet finally has a butler: it pays, swaps, and guards — you just live.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the slogan to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

A butler is a fresh image nobody else in the field uses: service, discretion, and competence in one word, instantly clear to a newcomer. The three verbs — pays, swaps, guards — say exactly what the agent does, and the tail "you just live" gives the line its rhythm and its promise: the wallet works so you don't have to. It avoids the stock crypto lines about keys, moons, and bags, and stays honest: the agent acts, the human lives.
